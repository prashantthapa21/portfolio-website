About Me

A learner with a practical mindset.

I am Prashant LMC Thapa, a BSc CSIT student from Jhapa, Nepal, currently in my 6th semester at ShreeYantra College. My academic journey began with school-level education and a strong interest in mathematics, science, and problem-solving, which later shaped my path toward technology and data.

My current focus is to become a professional Data Analyst while preparing for data-related opportunities and continuing to build practical skills with a long-term interest in AI and machine learning. I am learning, improving, and documenting the journey with honesty and consistency.
