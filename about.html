About Me

Learning with purpose, building with clarity.

I am Prashant LMC Thapa, a BSc CSIT student from Jhapa, Nepal, currently studying at ShreeYantra College. My academic journey began with SEE from Phoenix English School, Kamal-4, Jhapa, where I completed my school-level education with a GPA of 3.6 in the batch of 2021 AD (2078 BS).

I then completed my SLC +2 Science at Wonderland Boarding English School, Kamal-3, Jhapa, with a GPA of 3.20 in the batch of 2023 AD (2079 BS). I am now continuing my higher education in BSc CSIT at ShreeYantra College, where I started in 2023 AD (2080 BS), while learning Data Analysis through practical projects and real-world problem solving.

Alongside my studies, I work as a part-time secondary mathematics teacher at Smarika School, where I have been teaching since 2025. This role has strengthened my communication, consistency, and discipline—qualities that I carry into my growing career in technology.

My current focus is to become a professional Data Analyst while preparing for data-related opportunities and continuing to build practical skills with a long-term interest in AI and machine learning. I am learning, improving, and documenting the journey with honesty and consistency.
